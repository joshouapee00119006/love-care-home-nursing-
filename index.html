📘 How do I book a nurse? +

Steps:
1. Go to Home page
2. Fill Full Name, Phone (with code), Email, Service (e.g. Elderly Care)
3. Tap GPS Auto - allow location
4. Choose Hourly / Days / Weeks / Months
- Hourly: How many hours today? e.g. 5 hours
- Days: How many days? e.g. 7 days + 8 hrs/day = 56 hrs total
- Weeks: e.g. 2 weeks = 14 days = 112 hrs (if 8hrs/day)
- Months: e.g. 1 month = 30 days = 240 hrs
5. Your offer must be >= Legal Minimum (Ghana 15 GHS/hr)
6. Click FIND NURSES NEAR ME (needs GPS)
7. Pick nurse on map or click RANDOM, then BOOK SELECTED
8. You get confirmation. Track in MY BOOKINGS.

⏱ How does Hourly / Days / Weeks / Months work? +

New System:
• Hourly: Client enters total hours needed at home. Example: 5 hours = 5 hrs total
• Daily: Enter number of days (1-90) + hours per day (4,6,8,10,12,24). Example: 3 days x 8 hrs = 24 hrs
• Weekly: Enter number of weeks. System counts 7 days per week. Example: 2 weeks x 8 hrs = 2*7*8 = 112 hrs
• Monthly: Enter months. Counts 30 days per month. Example: 1 month x 8 hrs = 240 hrs
Total Hours × Legal Rate = Minimum Pay. You must offer at least that.

💰 What is the legal minimum pay? Ghana 15 GHS/hr +

Ghana Law: 15 GHS per hour minimum for nurses. App automatically calculates:
Formula: Total Hours × 15 GHS = Legal Minimum Total
Example: 7 days x 8 hrs = 56 hrs → 56×15 = 840 GHS minimum
If you offer less, app blocks booking. This protects nurses. Rates for other countries are set by CEO and auto-converted to your currency.

📍 GPS / Map not showing nurses? +

1. Tap GPS Auto button in booking form
2. Allow browser location permission (Allow)
3. Wait until green badge shows: GPS: 5.603...
4. If fails: Type your area manually (e.g. Madina, Accra)
5. Set radius to 10km or 20km
6. Click FIND NURSES NEAR ME again
Tip: Use Chrome browser, turn on phone Location.

💳 How does payment and budget work? +

You enter Your Offer in budget box. Must be >= legal minimum shown in yellow box. Example: If minimum is 840 GHS, offer 840 or more (e.g. 1000). Higher offer gets nurse faster. After booking, status is pending_assignment. If no nurse picks in 10 minutes, app asks you to increase rate. CEO confirms booking in dashboard.

📋 How to track My Bookings? +

Scroll to MY BOOKINGS card on Home. Enter your phone (same you booked with) and tap LOAD. You see all your bookings with status: PENDING (waiting nurse), CONFIRMED (nurse accepted), CANCELLED, REFUND REQUESTED etc. You can cancel if still pending or confirmed.

❌ How to cancel and request refund? +

1. Load MY BOOKINGS
2. Click Cancel on your booking
3. After cancelled, click Refund button
4. Fill reason, MoMo number or Bank Account, Account Name
5. Submit. Status becomes refund_requested
6. CEO reviews and approves. Money sent to your MoMo/bank.
Contact: lovecareglobal@gmail.com / 0544121814 if delay.

👩⚕ I am a nurse, how to join? +

Tap Join in bottom menu. Fill Full Name, Phone, Password, tap GPS for your location, Years experience, Specialty (13 options). Register. Then Login via 🔑 Login. In dashboard, tap START DUTY to go online (your location appears on client map). You see New Offers. Tap Accept to confirm job. Use GROUP CHAT.
