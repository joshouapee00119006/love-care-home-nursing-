💡 HELP - Live-in vs Live-out

Live-Out: Nurse comes daily, goes home after shift. You pay hourly rate x total hours.

Live-In: Nurse stays in your house 24/7. You must provide feeding OR food money. Duration you enter (e.g. 2 weeks + 3 days) is converted to total hours: 1 day=24h, 1 week=7 days, 1 month=30 days.

Example: Live-in for 2 weeks = 14 days = 336 hours x 15 GHS = 5040 GHS minimum + food allowance if not feeding.
